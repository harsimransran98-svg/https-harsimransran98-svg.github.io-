Questions

How long does a Chrome extension take to build?

A focused single-feature extension usually takes 1–3 weeks; a larger product with accounts, sync and a backend takes longer. You get a written scope, milestones and timeline before any work starts.

Can you migrate my Manifest V2 extension to Manifest V3?

Yes. We move background pages to service workers, webRequest blocking to declarativeNetRequest where needed, and update permissions and storage — then test against the current Chrome release.

Will the extension also work on Firefox, Safari and Edge?

Edge runs Chrome extensions directly. Firefox usually needs small changes, and Safari needs an Xcode wrapper; we plan for this from the start so one codebase covers all of them.

Who owns the code?

You do. Once the project is paid in full, the source code, designs and store listings belong to you.
