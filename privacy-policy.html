01Who we are

Bazozo Studio (“Bazozo”, “we”, “us”) is a product & engineering studio based in India, operating the website bazozo.in. For anything in this policy, contact us at hello@bazozo.in or +91 73475 00770.

02What we collect

When you browse bazozo.in

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting or any tracking scripts. There are no sign-ups and no forms on the site.

When you contact us

If you email us, message us on WhatsApp or call, we receive what you send: typically your name, email address or phone number, company, and the details of your project.

When you become a client

To deliver and invoice work we keep project communication, files you share with us, billing details (name, address, GST number if applicable) and payment records. Card or bank details are handled by the payment provider, not stored by us.

04Data stored in your own browser

Some interactive demos (for example the browser-extension and WordPress-plugin simulators) remember your settings using your browser’s local storage, so a demo looks the same when you come back. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

05How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To plan, build, deliver and support your project.
  • To send invoices and meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • To protect our legal rights if a dispute arises.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we never sell, rent or trade it.

06Consent and legal basis

We process personal data in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000. We rely on the consent you give when you contact us, and on legitimate uses such as performing a contract you have asked for and complying with law. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us; this does not affect processing already carried out.

07Who we share it with

Only with service providers we need to run the studio, and only as much as they need:

  • Email and messaging providers (for example Google Workspace or WhatsApp by Meta) that carry our conversations.
  • Payment processors and banks that handle your payments.
  • Accountants or tax advisers, where required by law.
  • Authorities, if we are legally required to disclose information.

Some of these providers store data outside India. Where that happens we rely on their standard contractual and security commitments.

08How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted within 24 months. Client project and billing records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (currently up to 8 years), then deleted.

09Security

bazozo.in is served only over HTTPS with a strict Content Security Policy and no third-party tracking. Inside the studio we use access-controlled accounts and two-factor authentication where available. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect what you share with us.

10Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it, where we are not required to keep it by law;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf.

Write to hello@bazozo.in. We reply within 30 days.
