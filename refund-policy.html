Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026Applies to bazozo.in

Short version: cancel before work starts and you get your advance back. After work starts, you pay only for work done and get back any unused part of your advance. Bazozo sells services and digital work, not physical goods, so there is nothing to ship back.

01What this covers

This policy covers payments made to Bazozo Studio for design, development, video and related services. Bazozo does not sell physical products, so there are no physical returns. Questions: hello@bazozo.in.

02Cancelling before work starts

If you cancel in writing before we begin work on your project, we refund your advance in full, minus any non-refundable fees charged by the payment provider.

03Cancelling after work has started

If you cancel after kick-off, you pay for the work completed up to the date we receive your written notice, based on the milestones and rates in your Proposal.

  • If your advance is more than the value of work completed, we refund the difference.
  • If it is less, we invoice the balance.
  • Either way, we hand over all completed work once the final account is settled.

04Approved milestones

Milestones that you have reviewed and approved are accepted and are not refundable. Before approving, you can always ask for the revisions included in your Proposal.

05If something is not right

If a deliverable does not match the agreed scope, tell us and we will fix it, free of charge, within the 30-day warranty period described in our Terms of Service. Our first step is always to put it right; a refund is considered if we cannot.

06Digital deliverables cannot be returned

Code, designs, video and other digital work cannot be “returned” once delivered, because copies remain with you. This is why refunds are based on the work completed rather than on returning files.

07Third-party costs

Money spent on your behalf with third parties, such as domains, hosting, paid plugins, fonts, stock media, app-store or extension-store fees, is non-refundable once purchased, because we cannot recover it.

08How to request a refund

  • Email hello@bazozo.in with your project name, invoice number and the reason for your request.
  • We reply within 5 business days.
  • Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 7–10 business days. The time for the money to reach your account depends on your bank.

09Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or other applicable law in India.
