01 — About
HS
.

One builder, ten crafts, zero patience for generic work.

Harsimranjot Singh runs Bazozo, an independent studio that designs and builds across the full stack of modern digital work — marketing sites and web apps, video edits, browser and WordPress extensions, and native apps for Android, iOS, Windows and macOS.

The throughline across all of it is the same: understand what the thing is actually for, then build it with more craft than the brief asked for. No templated layouts, no stock animation packs — every project starts from the subject, not a theme file.

Based inIndia (remote, worldwide clients)
