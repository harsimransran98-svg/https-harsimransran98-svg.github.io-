01 — About
BZ
.

Product & Engineering Studio. Zero patience for generic work.

Bazozo Studio is a Product & Engineering Studio led by Harsimranjot Singh. We design and build across four core pillars: web & e-commerce, browser extensions & plugins, cross-platform apps, and brand film & motion.

The throughline across all of it is the same: understand what the thing is actually for, then build it with more craft than the brief asked for. No templated layouts, no stock animation packs — every project starts from the subject, not a theme file.

Based inIndia (remote, worldwide clients)
FocusWeb, extensions, apps, film & motion
ApproachDesign + build, end to end
AvailabilityOpen for select projects
