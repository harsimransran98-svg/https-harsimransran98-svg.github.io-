The story behind the work

Salt & Cedar serves a single seasonal tasting menu, changed monthly. The website mirrors the dining pace: one course revealed at a time, with the ingredient story behind each dish.

Reservations are the only call to action on the entire site — there's no delivery menu, no merchandise, no distraction.
