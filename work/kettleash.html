The story behind the work

Kettle & Ash roasts twice a week, in batches small enough that the roaster tastes every one personally. The site was built to teach before it sells — a brew guide first, a shop second.

Every bean page includes origin notes, roast date, and the exact grind/ratio the roastery recommends, because a bad pour ruins good beans.
