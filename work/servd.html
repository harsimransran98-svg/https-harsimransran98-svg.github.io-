About this build

The waiter is the menu now.

Servd is a QR table-ordering system for restaurants, cafés and food courts, designed and built by Bazozo Studio as a working sample. Every table gets a QR code; guests scan it, order and pay from their own phone, and the kitchen sees every ticket the moment it is placed.

  • Guests browse an illustrated menu with veg and spice markers, add notes for the chef, follow their order live, call staff and pay by UPI.
  • The kitchen runs a live ticket board with timers that turn amber and red, switches dishes to sold out in one tap and handles waiter calls.
  • The owner sees revenue by hour, average bill and top dishes for the day.

This demo runs entirely in your browser: orders are saved on your device only and payments are simulated. The production version runs on a server with real QR codes per table, payments, printers and multi-outlet reporting.
