About

The story behind the work

Cast Iron runs six café locations across the city, each with its own hours and seating quirks. Rather than a generic locations list, the site centers on a live map with real-time status per café.

Every location page includes seating capacity, wifi policy, and whether it's currently a good spot for a laptop session or a quick coffee.
