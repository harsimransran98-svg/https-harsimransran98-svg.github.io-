About

The story behind the work

Marlow & Finch is a single-location independent bookshop, and every recommendation on the site comes from a named staff member, not an algorithm. Reading lists are curated by mood and season, not bestseller rank.

Every book page includes the staff member's actual handwritten note about why it made the list.
