The story

Typographic Reveals

Blackletter publishes literary fiction and poetry in small print runs, and every book's page is typeset in the actual typeface and layout used inside the printed edition.

Cover reveals unfold as typographic animation rather than a static image, because the press's reputation is built on typesetting craft as much as editorial taste.
