Aftercare.

The full guide ships with every booking confirmation. Here's the short version.

2h

Keep it wrapped

Leave the film on for two hours, then wash with lukewarm water and fragrance-free soap.

3×

Thin layers

A rice-grain of balm, three times a day. More is not better.

14d

No soaking

No baths, pools or sea for two weeks. Showers are fine.

∞

SPF forever

Sun fades ink faster than anything else. SPF 50 on healed work.
