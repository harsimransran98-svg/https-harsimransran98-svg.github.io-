About this build

A calmer waiting room.

Pulseline is a booking and queue system for clinics and multi-doctor practices, designed and built by Bazozo Studio as a working sample. Patients stop waiting in the lobby: they book, pay and track their turn from home, and the reception desk runs the day from one screen.

  • Patients book a doctor, pay by UPI, follow a live token queue, join video consults, receive e-prescriptions and get WhatsApp reminders.
  • Reception calls the next token, sees every doctor’s schedule and patient history, and new bookings appear on their own.
  • Doctors write prescriptions from quick-pick medicines and send them straight to the patient’s phone.

This demo runs entirely in your browser: data stays on your device and payments are simulated. The production version runs on a server with real accounts, payments, WhatsApp messages and secure video.
