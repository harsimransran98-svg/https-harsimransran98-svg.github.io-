About this build

One platform, many academies.

Lectern is a multi-tenant online classes platform (B2B2C SaaS) designed and built by Bazozo Studio as a working sample. Coaching institutes and academies sign up on a plan, upload their lectures, and their students get an app branded with the academy's own name and colour.

  • Students log in with an academy code, watch free lectures, unlock paid ones and join live classes with chat.
  • Academy admins build playlists, add YouTube lectures, go live and move students between Free and Paid.
  • The platform owner sees every academy, switches plans between Basic and Pro and adds new academies.

This demo runs entirely in your browser: changes are saved on your device only and payments are simulated. The production version runs on a server with real accounts, payments and live streaming, as an installable app for students. Use “Reset demo” at the top to start fresh.
