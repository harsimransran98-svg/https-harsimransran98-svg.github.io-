About

The story behind the work

Nordlys designs buildings around light before they design around form. The portfolio mirrors that priority: each project is presented as a slow parallax reveal, letting structure and shadow do the talking.

No stock renders, no generic hero videos — just the studio's own completed work, organized the way a visitor would actually walk through a finished building.
