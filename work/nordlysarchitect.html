A.03 — Materials

Concrete, birch, glass. Nothing else.

Board-marked concrete

Cast against rough-sawn spruce so the plinth carries the grain of the timber above it.

Birch cladding

Untreated, left to silver. Every board is numbered so it can be replaced in place.

Triple glazing

Low-iron glass so the light inside stays the colour of the light outside.
