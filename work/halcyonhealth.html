The story behind the work

Halcyon Health is a small wellness clinic focused on longer, unhurried appointments. The booking site mirrors that pace: slow transitions, generous whitespace, no urgency banners.

Every practitioner's page includes their actual approach and typical session length, so patients know what to expect before booking.
