About

The story behind the work

The Quiet Room is a meditation app built around short, structured ten-minute sessions rather than open-ended tracks. The landing page carries a faint ambient loop, optional and easy to mute, that hints at the in-app experience.

Every session category is described by structure and intent, not vague mood language — breath-focused, body-scan, or sleep-wind-down.
