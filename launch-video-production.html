Questions

What do you need from us to make a launch video?

Access to the product (or screenshots), your key message and any brand assets. We write the script and storyboard for your approval before production starts.

How long does a launch video take?

Usually 1–3 weeks from approved script, depending on length and how much animation it needs.

Can you deliver versions for social media?

Yes — vertical, square and widescreen cuts can be included in the scope.

Who owns the final video?

You do. Once the project is paid in full, the final files are yours.
