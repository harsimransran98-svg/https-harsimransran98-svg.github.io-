Ledgerly

The envelope method, without the envelopes. Ledgerly gives every pound a job the day you are paid, so you always know what is safe to spend. Scroll the story and the phone shows each step.

01 · chapter

Every pound has a job

Rent, food, fun, savings: each envelope shows what is left, as notes peeking out of the top.

02 · chapter

Payday, sorted in seconds

One tap shares your pay across envelopes using last month’s plan. Watch the notes fly in.

03 · chapter

Spend from the right envelope

Log a purchase against an envelope and it empties a little. No spreadsheets, no guessing.

04 · chapter

Borrow from fun, not from rent

Overspent on food? Move money from another envelope and the red stamp disappears.
