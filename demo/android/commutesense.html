CommuteSense

CommuteSense reads live vehicle positions and learns how your line usually misbehaves, then tells you exactly when to leave. Scroll the story and the phone follows along.

01 · chapter

Know when to leave

Not just the timetable: a leave-by time that already includes the delay your line is likely to have this morning.

02 · chapter

See the delay before it hits

Every tram on the line, live. Stretches running late glow amber and red, so you can see trouble coming two stops away.

03 · chapter

Take the faster way

When your usual route slips, CommuteSense ranks the alternatives by when you will actually arrive, with an honest confidence score.

04 · chapter

Get a nudge, not a nag

One quiet notification when it is time to go. If the delay grows, it updates the time instead of buzzing again.
