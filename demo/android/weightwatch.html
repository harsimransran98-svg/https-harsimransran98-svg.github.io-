WeightWatch

Daily weight jumps around by a kilo for no reason at all. WeightWatch shows the trend underneath it, so the number you look at is the one that actually means something.

Ten seconds a day

02 · chapter

The trend, not the noise

A smoothed line runs through the daily dots. It moves slowly, the way your body actually does.

03 · chapter

More than one number

Waist and body fat often change when the scale does not. Track them side by side.

Set a goal and WeightWatch projects when you will reach it at your current pace.
