FocusJar

A Pomodoro timer you can see. Every focused minute drops a marble into the jar; finish the session and the lid goes on. The phone is live, and demo speed fills a jar in seconds.

  1. Start a focus session
  2. Pick a task colour
  3. Pause and resume
  4. Fill and seal a jar
  5. Look at your shelf
