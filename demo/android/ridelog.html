RideLog

RideLog turns your phone into a trip computer: route, speed, lean angle and fuel, logged automatically for every ride. Hit start and take a mountain pass.

  1. Start a ride
  2. Hit 30° of lean
  3. Log a fill-up
  4. Open a past trip
  5. Compare fuel economy
