About Wallpaperly

Scheduled wallpaper rotation from local folders. Wallpaperly is a macOS app concept designed and built by Bazozo Studio to show the kind of macOS and Windows desktop utilities we ship — the screens above are a working, interactive build, and the brand is fictional.
