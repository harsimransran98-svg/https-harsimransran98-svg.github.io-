Pulseform

Pulseform reads your heart rate from Apple Watch and tells you, in real time, whether you are in the zone you meant to train in. Most easy runs are run too hard; this fixes that.

  1. Start a workout
  2. Pick up the pace
  3. Hold your target zone for 10 s
  4. Switch the zone method
  5. Finish and see your summary
