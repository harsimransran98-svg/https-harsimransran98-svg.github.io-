Inkwell

Inkwell gives you a quiet page and one good question each day. Focus mode fades everything except the sentence you are writing, so you keep going instead of editing.

  1. Swipe to a new prompt
  2. Write thirty words
  3. Turn on focus mode
  4. Tag your mood
  5. Read a past entry
