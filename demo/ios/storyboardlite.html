Storyboard

Storyboard Lite turns a scene into numbered panels with shot size, lens, camera move and timing, then plays them back as an animatic. On the day it becomes a shot list sorted by setup.

  1. Change a shot’s framing
  2. Add a camera move
  3. Drag a panel to reorder
  4. Play the animatic
  5. Sort the shot list by setup
