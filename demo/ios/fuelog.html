Fuelog

Fuelog reads your pump receipt so you never type a number, then works out what every kilometre costs and reminds you before a service is due. Scroll the story to drive through it.

01 · chapter

Snap the receipt, skip the typing

Point the camera at the pump receipt. Fuelog reads litres, price and the odometer in a second.

02 · chapter

Know what every kilometre costs

Economy and cost per kilometre, averaged over your last fills so one odd tank does not skew it.

03 · chapter

Maintenance that tells you when

Oil, tyres and the MOT, each counting down by distance or date.

04 · chapter

How far until empty?

Your real economy turns the fuel left into kilometres you can actually drive.
