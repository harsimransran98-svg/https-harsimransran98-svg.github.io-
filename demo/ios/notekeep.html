Notekeep

Notekeep lets you write the way you would on paper, then turns your handwriting into text you can copy and search. The recogniser in this demo is real: print a word on the lines and press Convert.

01 · chapter

Paper that feels like paper

Ruled lines, pressure-sensitive ink and a highlighter that sits under your writing, not on top of it.

02 · chapter

Handwriting becomes text

Each letter is matched against a shape model, then a word list fixes the near misses. Press Show me to watch it happen.

03 · chapter

Scribble to delete

No eraser hunting. Scribble back and forth over a word, typed or handwritten, and it is gone.

04 · chapter

Search what you wrote

Every note is indexed as text, so a search finds the word inside your handwriting and highlights it.
