The story behind the build

ContainerFlow is a lightweight firefox extensions that does exactly what it says: manages multi-account containers with quick switching. No bloated settings screen, no background trackers, no upsell halfway through onboarding.

It installs in one click, respects your browser's resources, and stays out of the way until you actually need it — try the popup below.
