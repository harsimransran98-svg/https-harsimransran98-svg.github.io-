Questions

Do you build native or cross-platform apps?

It depends on the app. Many projects use one cross-platform codebase for iOS and Android; apps that need deep device features may be built natively. We recommend an approach in the written quote.

How long does an app take?

A focused first version usually takes 4–10 weeks depending on scope. The quote lists every milestone and its date before work starts.

Can you publish the app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes — we prepare the listings, screenshots and privacy details and handle submission under your developer accounts.

Who owns the app?

You do. Once the project is paid in full, the source code and designs are yours.
